# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who dictate, write, and control a Mac at speed. They need visible live speech feedback, dependable system actions, and a private-feeling tool that stays out of the way until called.

## Product Purpose

Sayso turns speech into accurate multilingual dictation or grounded desktop control from a macOS notch surface. Success is immediate state clarity, safe action execution, and frictionless return to the user's work.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, alive. Sayso takes visual cues from clinical audio hardware: dark, high-contrast, compact, and deliberately energetic only for speech state and primary actions.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboards, soft glass overlays, fuzzy shadows, dense settings walls, and decorative color without operational meaning.

## Design Principles

1. Speech state is legible at a glance.
2. The notch stays compact until useful context is needed.
3. Primary actions are obvious, destructive actions are unmistakable.
4. Desktop control earns trust through explicit grounded feedback.
5. Settings support the workflow instead of competing with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep text and controls high contrast, label icon-only controls for VoiceOver, respect macOS reduced-motion settings, and preserve multilingual dictation language choices.
