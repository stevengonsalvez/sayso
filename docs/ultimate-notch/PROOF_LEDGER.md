## Calculator module (Wave 4, branch notch-calculator, 2026-10-05)

- `CalculatorModule` (id `calculator`, no capabilities, on by default through `SaysoSettings.calculatorEnabled`): `evaluate(_:)` returns a `CalculatorResult` (expression, formatted text, value) or a `CalculatorError` with a message, keeps the last 10 results in memory (newest first) and publishes one `.completion` activity `12 × 3 = 36` (expression cut to 40 characters) with Copy and Dismiss for 10 s. The module's own scheduler job dismisses it (the activity also carries `expiresAfter`, so the app's expiry ticker agrees); a new result replaces the activity and re-arms the one job, and a timer callback already under way for an older notice changes nothing. Errors change neither history nor the notch. `copy(id)` writes the result text through `CalculatorPasteboardPort`, which has only `write`; the app adapter `PasteboardCalculatorPort` clears the general pasteboard and sets plain text, and is called only from Copy. Disable cancels the job and empties history (`retainedResources == 0`); evaluate and copy then refuse (`Calculator is off. Turn it on in Settings.`). Angles default to degrees.
- `CalculatorEngine`: tokenizer plus recursive-descent parser, no `NSExpression`. Precedence, parentheses, unary minus (also `−`), right-associative `^` binding tighter than unary minus, `*`, `×`, `·`, `x`, `X`, `/`, `÷`, percent (`15% of 80` = 12, `200 + 10%` = 220, `50% + 50%` = 1), `1,200.5` input (comma only before groups of exactly three digits after a first group of one to three not starting with 0), `pi`, `π`, `e`, `sqrt`, `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `ln`, `log`, `abs`, `round`; exact trig values at whole right angles (degrees reduced modulo 360 first, radians snapped only below a million quarter turns). Input over 500 characters is refused before tokenizing; nesting over 64 levels through one counter in `parseUnary`, which every recursive path passes. Every intermediate is checked: infinity is `Too large to show`, NaN `Not a real number`, a zero divisor `Cannot divide by zero`. Conversions `amount unit (in|to|as) unit` for length, mass, temperature, volume (US), speed and data size (decimal `kb` to `tb`, binary `kib` to `tib`, case-insensitive, so `mb` is a megabyte); conversions keep 12 significant digits, read cancellation noise as 0 and leave whole results exact. `CalculatorFormatter` shows at most 10 significant digits in the injected locale, whole numbers below 10^15 in full, scientific outside 1e-6 to 1e10, and `-0` as `0`.
